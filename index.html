    How each predictor moves the estimated probability away from the average prediction E[f(X)]. Red bars increase the probability; blue bars decrease it.

    Research use only. This tool estimates the probability that an adolescent who reports serious suicidal ideation has made a suicide attempt in the past 12 months, based on self-reported survey items. It does not predict future suicidal behavior and must not replace clinical assessment. A low estimate does not mean that an adolescent is safe. If someone is in immediate danger, call local emergency services. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

    About the model

    The calculator implements the final logistic regression (LR) model from the study “Development and validation of a machine learning model for identifying suicide attempts among adolescents with suicidal ideation: a national survey-based study.”

    Data sourceUS national Youth Risk Behavior Survey (YRBS), 2019, 2021, 2023 and 2025
    PopulationStudents aged 14–17 years in grades 9–12 reporting serious suicidal ideation in the past 12 months (n = 9,824)
    OutcomeSelf-reported suicide attempt (≥1) in the past 12 months; prevalence 41.0%
    DevelopmentTraining set n = 6,876 (70%); test set n = 2,948 (30%), stratified by outcome and survey year
    Predictors18 predictors selected by both LASSO and Boruta in the training set
    AlgorithmL2-penalized logistic regression (C = 0.1); selected among 8 algorithms with comparable discrimination and calibration as the least complex model
    Test-set performanceAUC-ROC 0.778 (95% CI 0.762–0.795); AUC-PRC 0.690; Brier score 0.188; Hosmer–Lemeshow P = 0.160
    Threshold0.36 (F1-optimal in training cross-validation): sensitivity 0.822, specificity 0.583, PPV 0.578, NPV 0.825 in the test set
    ExplanationPermutation SHAP values on the probability scale with a background sample of 200 training observations

    Important limitations

    • Predictors and outcome were measured concurrently; the model identifies past-year attempts and does not forecast future attempts.
    • All information is self-reported in an anonymous school-based survey; performance in clinical settings has not been evaluated.
    • The model was developed in US high school students and has not been externally validated.
    • Positive predictive value depends on prevalence and will be lower in populations with fewer attempts (see Table S9 of the paper).

    Model equation

    Probability = 1 / [1 + exp(−(β0 + Σ βixi))], with coefficients on the original scale of the predictors (Table S8 of the paper).
